# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to debt-disclosure-bounds 1.1.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23337976)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v1.1.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/debt-disclosure-bounds/>.

1. Corrected Recommendation 1 in §13. The archived text says the bound holds “of every year-by-year path for fixed-rate debt and continuously resetting floating debt”. That drops the condition of Corollary 7(ii). It should read: “of every path constant within years for fixed-rate debt refinanced into debt not refixing again before H, and for continuously resetting floating debt and reserves”. As the paper notes, claim (ii) does not extend to Treasury bills.

   The corollary is stated correctly; only the recommendation's summary is affected. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.

2. Corrected a comparison in §9. The archived text compares the frozen-principal inflation illustration of £7.1 billion with the OBR's £7.3 billion, but these figures are for different years. Table 9 gives £5.2 billion against the OBR's £7.3 billion in year 1, and £7.1 billion against £8.5–10.5 billion in years 2–5; the sentence should say so.

   Table 9 is correct. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
