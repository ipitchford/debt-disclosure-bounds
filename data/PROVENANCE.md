# Data provenance

The supplied source files were recorded as retrieved on 13 October 2026. SHA-256 checksums are in `INPUTS.sha256`; `scripts/check_inputs.py` checks all computational inputs. BoE and OBR originals remain private and are omitted from the public archive; see `EXTERNAL_INPUTS.md`. The public archive retains exact computational model snapshots and certificate/results linkage for offline checking. Source redistribution terms remain separate from the CC0 licence on original research.

Revision: a fresh ordinary-browser DMO export, `raw/dmo_official_gilts_20261012.xls`, was retrieved on 13 October with internal heading “Gilts in issue on 12 October 2026”. `scripts/verify_official_gilts.py` matches all 105 ISINs, types, redemption dates, nominal and inflation-uplifted amounts against the supplied XML. Thus the old transcription-authentication limitation below is resolved for these computational fields, not for reconstructed bill timing.
